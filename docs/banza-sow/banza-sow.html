01Overview

Banza is a Bengaluru consumer AI app: a personal AI Twin that connects to a user's shopping, streaming, email and social accounts to find better prices and earn money back. Following the Brand Audit and Identity Proposal, Aakar Labs will redesign the Banza identity so it reads as quietly capable, trustworthy and personal, rather than a reward app.

This document fixes what is included, how work and feedback are staged, and when payments fall due.

02Deliverables

03Process and milestones

Work runs in staged reviews so feedback lands early and large changes of direction do not appear late. Aakar Labs shares progress on a shared board (Figma or similar); Banza comments on each stage. Total effort is about 20 working days from kickoff.

StageWhat happensTarget date
KickoffIntro call, shared board and WhatsApp group set up, co-founder included.date
Directions2–3 creative directions for logo, mascot and colour. Banza picks one and comments.date
Draft 1Chosen direction developed: mark, icon set, type and palette. Comments round.date
Draft 2Motion states and playbook added; refinements applied. Comments round.date
Final deliveryAll files and the playbook handed over.date

Banza has a November marketing push, so Aakar Labs will share the icon and mark early where possible to support that work.

05Feedback and revisions

  • Banza gives one set of consolidated comments per stage, within 2 working days of each share.
  • Each deliverable includes two revision rounds. Further rounds are ₹10,000 each.
  • A change of creative direction after Directions is approved counts as new work and is quoted separately.

06Out of scope

  • UI components and app or website screen design.
  • Additional animation states, extra mascot poses beyond the agreed set, and illustration or packaging work.
  • Development, copywriting beyond the headline rewrites in the audit, and third-party licensing costs.

07Ownership

Full rights in the final approved deliverables pass to Banza on receipt of final payment. Aakar Labs may show the work in its portfolio after Banza's public launch of the new identity.
