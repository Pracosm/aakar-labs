01Purpose and deliverables

Purpose of the engagement

Develop a distinctive, coherent visual identity for Banza, the AI Twin shopping app. The identity should look capable and interesting on first contact, be recognisable at small app and notification sizes, and support motion that shows the Twin working for the user. The Banza name and founder-supplied messaging remain unchanged.

Creative approach

  • Design from a fresh starting point; the existing logo does not need to be retained.
  • Present 2–3 exploratory routes before refining one selected direction.
  • Explore one canonical Twin character direction as part of the mark and icon work. This is not a standalone mascot system.
  • The master identity must accommodate Banza Save, Validate, Activate and Cash without designing separate product logos.

ALogo, wordmark and mark

  • Final primary wordmark and symbol/monogram, developed as one coherent identity.
  • Horizontal and stacked lockups, including a version with Banza's founder-provided working tagline.
  • Approved light, dark and one-colour variations, clear-space and minimum-size rules.
  • 24 px readability, monochrome notification and WhatsApp-preview suitability checks; clear visual interpretation review.
  • Master vector and source deliverables: SVG, transparent PNG exports and native editable design files.

BApp icon and variations

  • 2–3 exploratory icon candidates for Banza to use in its Google Play listing experiment. These are exploratory design options, not three complete production-ready icon systems.
  • One final approved app icon, including appropriate light, dark and monochrome variations, supplied in standard export formats suitable for use across major mobile platforms.
  • General visual guidance for potential seasonal or festive adaptations of the approved app icon, included as a short part of the brand guidelines. Actual seasonal icon designs are not included.
  • General visual benchmarking against relevant consumer app identities during the design process, as part of the studio's internal creative evaluation. No separate competitor analysis, research report or benchmarking presentation is included.
Acceptance of icon scopeThe studio is responsible for design assets and standard exports. Implementation, store submission, technical integration and running experiments are Banza's responsibility.

02Production deliverables

CThree core motion states

StateRole and asset
IdleA calm default presence; loop and static fallback.
Thinking / WorkingPrimary signature state communicating work in progress; timing should build naturally toward completion; loop or defined transition and static fallback.
Done / SuccessShort, restrained success confirmation; playback and static fallback.
  • Motion principles sheet covering durations, timing, easing and intended transitions between the three states.
  • Code-based implementations of the approved animations, developed directly in code. The studio delivers the animation source code in one mutually agreed technology or format, together with previews or demonstrations of the animations.
  • A fully wired state machine and optional Launch, Listening and Error animations are not included. Integration into Banza's application, adaptation to its existing codebase, ongoing debugging and engineering implementation are excluded.

DColour and typography

  • Primary, secondary and semantic colours (success, warning, error, information), with light/dark tuning and darker text-safe counterparts.
  • Approved accessibility-tested colour pairs targeting WCAG AA 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for relevant non-text components.
  • Brand and interface typeface recommendations, hierarchy and type scale, numeral/tabular-figure treatment, recommended Indian-script and Arabic fallback typefaces with usage guidelines, and mobile font embedding licence references. These are recommendations and guidelines; custom typeface design and exhaustive multilingual testing are not included.

EShort brand playbook and design tokens

  • Approximately 15–25 pages in PDF, plus editable design source, covering the identity rationale; logo, icon, colour and typography usage; examples of correct and incorrect application; motion states and specs.
  • Short application-guidance page for store graphics, notifications and share cards. Production of the actual campaign or store artwork is excluded.
  • W3C-style design tokens supplied as structured JSON files containing the relevant identity design tokens (colour, type, spacing, radii and motion). Tokens are supplied as files only; integration into Banza's codebase and engineering support are not included. This does not include a UI component library.

05Approvals, revision rules and dependencies

How feedback and approval will work

One approver, one consolidated response. Banza shall nominate one decision-maker to collect internal comments and provide a single written response per milestone. Meeting notes are shared as a short decision record. A verbal preference is not an approval unless confirmed in writing (email or the designated project channel).

Approval cut-offs and deemed approval

All written approvals on 22 October, 24 October and 8 November are due by 11:59 PM IST on that date. If Banza provides no written feedback or approval by a stated deadline, the deliverable is deemed approved for that stage and work proceeds. For the 24 October and 8 November gates, deemed approval occurs at 11:59 PM IST.

Revision allowance

The agreed core bundle allows up to two structured rounds of consolidated in-scope feedback per major deliverable, provided they occur before the applicable approval gate and within this schedule. The moodboard and initial 2–3 route review are exploratory selection meetings, not a commitment to develop all routes into complete identity systems. An unselected route is not an additional final deliverable.

For the logo, the scheduled feedback opportunities are the 22 October route selection and the refinement review culminating in 24 October approval. Banza sends one consolidated note on the selected route by 23 October, 12:00 noon IST, and Aakar Labs presents the refined logo on 24 October. Late feedback reduces what can be shown on the 24th but does not move the lock. No unused logo revision allowance carries beyond the 24 October lock. Later icon, colour, type, motion and playbook revisions may not alter the approved logo geometry.

Binding creative gates24 October: approved logo, mark and canonical identity geometry are frozen. 8 November: all remaining design and documentation are final and frozen. Corrections to corrupted exports or objectively incorrect files do not reopen creative approval; substantive changes require separate written agreement.

Feedback turnaround and weekends

  • Banza provides founder questionnaire responses and key materials by the stated deadlines.
  • All key reviewers commit to the 18 October, 24 October and 8 November weekend approvals.
  • Diwali. Both parties confirm availability on 8 November 2026 despite the festival. If either cannot attend, written sign-off is due by 7 November, 6:00 PM IST, or by 9 November, 12:00 noon IST. The 10 November handover date is unchanged.
  • Design reviews require one consolidated response by the deadlines shown; the production plan assumes rapid, usually same-day or next-day decisions.
  • Client-caused delays, unanswered technical questions, late content or new requests may move dependent milestones. Both parties will agree any revised dates in writing; a fixed handover cannot be guaranteed without timely approvals.
